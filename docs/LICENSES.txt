What's that muscle? and the licences of what is in it

The page's own code: Apache-2.0.

Anatomy facts: from English Wikipedia's articles (their infoboxes, opening sentences,
categories and redirects), CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ .
Each fact's card links the article revision it came from. The data is shared alike.

Exercises and their steps: free-exercise-db, https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db ,
released into the public domain (the Unlicense).

The 3D body: BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under
CC Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ ,
from https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/ (release 4.0). Its meshes were
decimated with Blender and packed for the page; its part names are the Foundational
Model of Anatomy's. The files' own headers still name the older CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan
licence; the database's licence page has said CC BY 4.0 since 2025-02-27.

The 3D body's nerves of the trunk and limbs, and its lymph nodes: Z-Anatomy, "Z-Anatomy -
The libre 3D atlas of anatomy - CC-BY-SA 4.0", made from "BodyParts3D - The Database
Center for Life Science", https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Unity-app_Z-Anatomy (commit
938896b), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Read from its FBX files,
placed in BodyParts3D's frame and decimated. The models Z-Anatomy took in under
non-commercial licences (the inner ear, the kidney) are left out.
